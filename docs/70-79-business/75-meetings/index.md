## 2026-11-07 answers \{#2026-11-07-answers\}

The owner answered some of the nineteen questions in the brief of 7 November 2026.

- The brief shows no answer for questions 4, 8 and 10 to 19.
- The table below quotes each answer as written in the brief.

| Question | Owner answer |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Does Red Cab need registration under the Travel Agency Act? | "Saas platform only" |
| 2. Who confirms the permit rules and the seat limit between the two permits? | "Admin checks manually and approve the permits" |
| 3. Does the owner sign off the decisions? | "Decided to be removed." |
| 5. Must providers be registered companies with a permit, a matching vehicle and insurance? | "business permit, insurance, MLIT permit" |
| 6. Does the owner confirm the four listing service types? | "Approved." |
| 7. Who reviews provider applications and records licence expiry dates? | "prefer to have a structured automated checking for permits." |
| 9. Are cancel and refund, booking emails and the licence pause required before launch? | "Required. Might need to review the policy how to make profit from the cancellation (e.g. 5days before is free, for tour 14 days)" and "prefer the strict preset of cancellation policy" |

- The principal engineer reports that the owner confirmed that "SaaS platform only" needs no travel agency registration.
  - No dated record of this confirmation exists yet.
  - The note of 14 November 2026 tests this answer against the law.
