## TL;DR \{#catalog-overview}

- **Catalog** owns listings, geography presentation, discovery, and provider listing configuration.
- Normative rules for the [Catalog revamp](/docs/60-69-initiatives/61-2026/catalog-revamp-2026/) live here: [Catalog decisions](/docs/30-49-domains/catalog/catalog-decisions/#catalog-decisions-tldr), [Naming and legacy aliases](/docs/30-49-domains/catalog/naming-and-legacy-aliases/#naming-tldr), plus `requirements/`, `models/`, `architecture-decisions/` as they land.
- Global invariants (pricing authority, seats, snapshots) remain in [Business rules](/docs/70-79-business/business-rules/invariants).
- Legacy `FR-CAT-*` requirements stay under [Functional requirements — CAT](/docs/70-79-business/requirements/functional-requirements/cat) until fully superseded or moved to [Legacy](/docs/80-89-legacy/).
